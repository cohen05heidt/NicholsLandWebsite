# Setting up listing management — what we need from Nichols

This is a one-time setup so your staff can add and remove land listings on the
new website themselves, without a developer.

There are **two free accounts** to create and **two things to send back**. No
payment details are involved at any stage, and neither account needs a paid
plan now or later.

---

## 1. A GitHub account for whoever manages listings

This is the login they will use to add and remove tracts.

1. Go to <https://github.com/signup>.
2. Sign up with a work email address. It's free.
3. Send back **the username they chose.**

We'll then invite that username to the website, which is what gives them
permission to change listings. Access can be removed at any time, instantly.

If more than one person will be adding listings, each needs their own account —
don't share one login. Send us every username.

---

## 2. A Cloudflare account owned by Nichols

This one is not a login your staff will ever use. It runs a small free service
that makes the "Sign in with GitHub" button on the admin page work.

It matters that **Nichols owns this account**, not an individual contractor. If
it lived in someone else's personal account and they moved on, your staff would
one day be unable to log in with no obvious explanation.

1. Go to <https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up>.
2. Sign up using a shared company address — something like `info@nicholsland.net`
   rather than one person's inbox. The free plan is all this needs, forever.
3. Send back **the email address used**, and let us know when it's created.

We'll walk whoever holds that account through a five-minute setup screen-share,
or send exact click-by-click steps — whichever you prefer. Nothing needs to be
installed on any computer.

---

## What happens after that

Your staff go to the admin page, click **Sign in with GitHub**, and see a list
of every tract. They can:

- Add a new listing with a form — tract name, acreage, price, county, land type,
  map location, description, photos, and PDF maps or plats
- Edit anything on an existing listing
- Mark a tract Under Contract or Sold
- Delete a listing entirely

Any change appears on the public website about a minute later, with no further
step and nobody to notify. Every change is recorded, so anything done by
accident can be undone.

---

## Questions people usually ask

**Do we have to pay for any of this?** No. Both accounts are free at the volume
this site will ever use, and there is no upgrade prompt to fall into.

**Can someone break the website by entering something wrong?** No. If a listing
is filled in incorrectly, the site keeps showing the last good version of the
listings rather than publishing the mistake.

**What if we accidentally delete a tract?** It can be restored. Every version of
every listing is kept.

**Do photos need resizing first?** No, upload them as they come off the camera
or out of the MLS export.
