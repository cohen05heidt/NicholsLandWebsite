Transitional Property Management

Land considered to be of a higher and better use than a traditional timberland investment requires a different set of considerations. Southeastern Agri-Business works closely with Nichols Land & Investment to ensure proper planning and management of tracts that will one day be developed or converted — from zoning concerns to erosion management.

Should a property develop from a timber investment into a commercial investment, the transition from timberland management to sales and commercial leasing is seamless.

Our History

Providing expertise and guidance for 50 years

Nichols Land & Investment Co. has been growing to serve the real estate investment and management goals of our clients since 1976.

Founded by Carl Nichols in 1976, the business started by serving local investors who demanded expert advice and guidance with their agricultural and timberland investments.

Today, five decades later, we are a full-service real estate company specializing in timberland, rural land, recreational land and all classes of commercial property including office, retail, industrial and special use. Our growth has allowed us to better serve the needs of our local clients as well as those of our large and active base of European clients.

Nichols Land & Investment Co. is focused on providing high-quality services for the acquisition, ownership and disposition of your real estate investments, and strives to meet the long-term investment needs of our clients.
